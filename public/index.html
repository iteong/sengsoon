Careers

Your licence. We help you earn it.

Big firms hire graduates into one narrow role. In a team our size you see the whole job, from cable tray to final claim, and you learn it from the people running it. The goal is for you to become a Licensed Electrical Worker.

  1. On site

    Learn the work with our supervisors

    Installation, testing and commissioning on live projects: condominiums, healthcare facilities, commercial buildings and rail sites.

  2. In the office

    Learn how projects are run

    Progress claims, drawings, coordination with main contractors and consultants, and how jobs are priced, alongside the Managing Director.

  3. Licence

    Become our Licensed Electrical Worker

    Work towards the EMA Electrical Technician licence, which lets you take charge of installations up to 500 kVA. If you perform well, we pay for your first attempt at the EN033 course and the licence application, and give you the practical experience it requires. Your first three years follow a development plan, with salary reviews at key milestones.

Questions

Common questions.

Quick answers for building managers, contractors and owners looking for an electrical contractor in Singapore.

Is Seng Soon a registered electrical contractor?

Yes. We are registered with the Building and Construction Authority under workhead ME05 (Electrical Engineering) at grade L1, certified bizSAFE Star and ISO 9001:2015, and have been an SMRT registered contractor since 1998.

Can you renew our EMA electrical installation licence?

Yes. Our in-house Licensed Electrical Worker can act as the LEW for your installation, handle the application or annual renewal, and carry out the required inspection and testing. Non-domestic installations above 45 kVA need this licence, and renewals should be filed at least a month before expiry.

Do you work directly with MCSTs?

Yes. We contract directly with management corporations for switchboard upgrades and other common-area works, and we have held servicing contracts with some MCSTs for more than ten years.

What size of project do you take on?

We take on everything from repairs, servicing and licence renewals to multi-million-dollar installations. Our largest single contract was $6 million, and we partner with other contractors on the largest developments. Every site is led by one of our own supervisors.

Do you work as a subcontractor to main contractors?

Most of our new-build work is as the electrical subcontractor to a main contractor or M&E firm, on residential, healthcare, education and commercial projects.

Where are you located?

Our office is at Block 3006 Ubi Road 1, #02-358, Kampong Ubi Industrial Estate, Singapore 408700. We work on sites across Singapore, including underground rail sites.
